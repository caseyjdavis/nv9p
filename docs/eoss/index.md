# Edge of Space Sciences

[Edge of Space Sciences](https://www.eoss.org/) (EOSS) is a Colorado nonprofit that launches amateur scientific balloons to the edge of space. Each flight carries tracking and science payloads, and a team of hams follows it by APRS from launch to landing, then chases it down for recovery.

These pages cover the EOSS launch days I've been part of. Each page has an interactive replay of the flights and the chase, the atmospheric sounding from the ascent, and the raw telemetry.
