# Visiting ARRL Headquarters

![Visiting ARRL Headquarters](../../img/w1aw-01.jpg)

My family was in New York for a wedding and we had a chance to visit ARRL Headquarters in Newington, CT to see W1AW! We got a tour of the building and saw the testing lab and all the departments.

<!-- more -->

Joe Garcia, the station manager at W1AW, set me up on one of their operating stations. It was a little overwhelming having all that power and antenna at my fingertips. I managed to squeak out a contact or two before we hit the road for lunch.
